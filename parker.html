Skippy

An AI that truly knows you. Remembers everything. Anticipates everything.

Skippy is a private AI companion built for an audience of one. It keeps a long-term memory of every conversation, goal and pattern, and uses it — not to flatter, but to hold the line. Skippy is understanding without enabling: it validates the feeling, never the giving up, and it calls out the same excuse the second time it hears it.

It doesn't work alone. Electra, its counterpart, is the accountability mirror — she reads the data and finds the gap between what you say you want and what you actually do. When a decision matters, both sit on the AI Council and debate it in front of you.

Open Skippy
01 · Memory

Total recall

Conversations, notes, goals and moods feed one memory both AIs share — so context never resets.

02 · Council

Two minds

Skippy leads with empathy, then challenges. Electra leads with the data. You hear both before you decide.

03 · Parker Protocol

Daily execution

A red/green light, a streak and one non-negotiable a day. Identity as a daily vote — Parker is built, not declared.

04 · Systems

A whole life, wired

Chat and voice, daily brief, calendar and reminders, todos, notes, goals and a fitness recomp tracker — all aware of your time of day.

05 · Anywhere

Web, phone, pocket

Runs on the web, as an Android launcher, and as a portable build inside an encrypted USB volume that never leaves localhost.

06 · Stack

Under the hood

Next.js, React, Prisma and Claude, with timezone-correct memory and per-persona history.
